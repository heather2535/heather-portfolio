My Story

I grew up in the Bay Area and moved to Boston to study graphic design at Boston University. Typography taught me how to guide someone’s attention. Studying psychology made me curious about what people notice and where they get stuck.

That curiosity led me to product design. I’ve designed tools for researchers, recruiters, and people looking for ALS care resources. Now back in the Bay Area, I’m building Route Tracker, an iPhone app that helps drivers understand what their trips cost.
