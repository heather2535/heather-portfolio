Plant Care AI

Helping urban dwellers nurture healthy indoor plants effortlessly.

Role
Design Strategist, UX Researcher, UI/UX Designer
Team
Individual project
Tools
Figma, Adobe XD, ProtoPie, Miro
Focus
AI diagnostics, research, accessibility, mobile UX

Inspiration

Plant Care AI began with a question: what if your houseplants had their own AI caretaker? For people living in apartments, remembering watering schedules, diagnosing leaf problems, and finding the right lighting can be surprisingly difficult.

I designed a mobile-first concept bringing together photo-based AI diagnostics, personalized care schedules, and community support in a single plant companion.

The problem

Generic plant care tips and static reminders don't necessarily reflect a plant's current condition. When leaves begin to yellow or droop, plant owners may not know what's wrong until the damage has progressed.

How might we help indoor plant owners diagnose, care for, and feel confident about their plants through a personalized, proactive experience?

Early interface exploration and usability feedback from the original case study.

Research

I explored the challenges of plant ownership through surveys, interviews, and contextual inquiry with eight plant owners living in small apartments. I focused on the gap between wanting to care for plants and knowing how to respond when something goes wrong.

Design direction

Photo-based diagnostics

Help plant owners investigate visible symptoms and understand possible next steps instead of relying only on general advice.

Personalized care

Bring care scheduling and plant monitoring into a more proactive, approachable experience.

Community and engagement

Explore ways to make learning and caring for plants feel less isolating and easier to sustain.

Process & iteration

I moved from research and early sketches into wireframes and interface explorations, refining the experience around clarity, feedback, and the needs of novice plant owners.

Selected research and design process imagery from the original Plant Care AI project.

Takeaway

Plant Care AI brought together user research, AI-assisted product thinking, mobile interaction design, and visual prototyping. It challenged me to translate a broad everyday problem into a more focused, human-centered product concept.

This is a design case study and concept; it does not claim a publicly released app or measured adoption.
