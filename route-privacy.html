Route Tracker Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Route Tracker is an iOS app developed by Heather Davies. It uses location to help you plan routes, see your position and record drives. When you’re signed in, your saved drives, including precise GPS paths, sync to your Route Tracker account through Supabase.

Information we use · Location and motion · Service providers · Retention and deletion · Contact

Information we collect and why

  • Account and profile. When you create an account, we use your email address, account identifier and the name or username you provide to sign you in, verify your email, help you recover access and keep your saved information associated with your account. Supabase handles email and password authentication. You can also sign in with Apple, which supplies an account identifier and may provide your name and email address or an Apple private relay address. A profile photo you choose to add can be saved with your profile.
  • Drives and location. With location permission, the app uses your current location for the map, route planning and drive recording. Recorded trip data includes precise GPS coordinates and timestamps, start and end locations, distance and duration. The app uses these details to display your trips and calculate driving statistics and estimated costs.
  • Routes, vehicles and settings. The app may store saved routes, destination searches, recent places, vehicle details, fuel economy estimates, trip notes, and preferences you enter or select. It uses them to restore your settings and saved work, plan routes and estimate trip costs.
  • Account sync. Supabase hosts your account and cloud copy. While you’re signed in, the app can upload your saved drives and GPS tracks, routes, vehicles, profile information and preferences so your account can restore them. Changes made offline may remain on your iPhone until they sync.
  • Support and service logs. If you contact us, we receive your contact details and any information you choose to send so we can respond. When you use online features or visit this policy page, hosting or feature providers may receive request and connection details such as your IP address for website or service operation.

Location, motion and your choices

Location powers the live map, route planning, distance measurements and recorded drive paths. When you enable drive recording and allow background location, the app can use location while the screen is locked or another app is open. With Motion & Fitness permission, motion signals help the app recognize vehicle travel for automatic recording.

You can turn off automatic recording in the app. In iPhone Settings, you can change Route Tracker’s Location and Motion & Fitness permissions or turn off Precise Location. Route planning can start from a manually entered address. Changing a permission stops or limits future use of that permission. Previously recorded and synced trips remain until you delete them.

When information goes to other services

  • Supabase provides authentication and cloud storage, including the account and synced information described above.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and Apple Maps services. Maps, routing, place lookup and fuel-station searches may use search terms and coordinates. Opening directions in Apple Maps shares the route details needed to open navigation.
  • Other map and data providers may process searches, coordinates, routing preferences or planned departure times. These include OpenStreetMap-based routing and search services such as Photon and Nominatim, and Open-Meteo for elevation. Vehicle and fuel estimates use vehicle or regional information from NHTSA, FuelEconomy.gov and AAA. Applicable screens may load fonts or imagery from Google Fonts or Wikimedia. Providers may receive connection details and keep logs under their own policies.
  • Services acting for Route Tracker receive information to provide their designated service. We require providers processing personal data on our behalf to protect it consistently with this policy and applicable requirements.

Route Tracker does not sell your drive history or use it for targeted advertising. If you export or share a backup, it can include precise location history, vehicles and settings. You choose the recipient or storage location and can delete those copies separately.

How long we keep information and how to delete it

We keep your active account information and synced records while your account is open, unless you delete them sooner. You can delete individual drives in the app and manage your saved routes and vehicles. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data, subject to device backups, but does not delete your account or its cloud copy.

To request account deletion, open Profile → Account → Delete account and confirm. When deletion completes, it removes your Route Tracker account and its saved app data from active cloud storage, clears that account’s app data from the iPhone used to request deletion, and signs you out. If you use Sign in with Apple, you may be asked to confirm with Apple. If you can’t complete the request, contact us at the address below.

Copies in your own exports or device backups are managed separately. Service backups and operational logs may retain data until their applicable retention periods expire. We may also keep information when required by law. Contact us if you want to ask how these exceptions apply to your data.

How we protect information

Route Tracker uses iOS storage protections, stores sign-in sessions in the iOS Keychain, and uses encrypted connections for online services. Cloud access controls associate synced records with the signed-in account. Protect any backups you export because they can reveal places you have visited.

Privacy requests and policy changes

To ask about your information or request access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal of consent where applicable, email violet6889@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity to respond. Route Tracker is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us.

When we update this policy, we’ll publish the revised text and effective date on this page.
