ROUTE

Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

ROUTE: Plan & Track is developed by Heather Davies. This policy explains how the iOS app handles information when you plan routes, record drives and view driving statistics.

Your drive history and preferences are stored locally on your device. Online features send the information needed to external map and data services. ROUTE does not require an account and does not include advertising or advertising tracking.

Information stored on your device

Depending on the features you use, ROUTE stores:

  • Recorded GPS coordinates and timestamps, drive distance and duration, start and end locations, and links between planned routes and recorded drives.
  • Saved routes, destination searches and recent places, route preferences, departure dates and times, and cached routes, elevation and station results.
  • Vehicles you enter or select, including year, make, model and fuel-economy estimates; optional display name; drive notes and edits; app settings and permission-related recording status.

Driving statistics and estimated fuel costs are calculated from available drive records, vehicle information and fuel-price data. ROUTE does not operate a developer-hosted account or drive-history synchronization service.

Location and motion permissions

ROUTE uses location to find your current starting point, plan routes and record drive paths and mileage. When you enable automatic drive recording and grant the relevant permissions, location may be used in the background, including while the screen is locked or another app is open. Motion & Fitness information helps detect vehicle travel.

You can change recording settings in ROUTE and manage Location and Motion & Fitness permissions in iPhone Settings. Turning off recording does not automatically delete previously saved drives. You can use route planning without enabling automatic drive recording.

Information sent to external services

Online features contact external providers. Requests may include place-search text, starting and destination coordinates, route coordinates, routing preferences, departure time where supported, vehicle year/make/model, or the region needed for fuel-price information. Providers also receive connection information such as your IP address and may retain request logs under their own policies.

  • Apple Maps / MapKit: maps, routing, place and fuel-station searches, and location names. Opening navigation in Apple Maps passes route information to that app.
  • Other routing and place-search services: available routing providers and OpenStreetMap-based services, including Photon and Nominatim, may process searches or route requests.
  • Open-Meteo: sampled route coordinates for terrain elevation.
  • U.S. government vehicle services: NHTSA for vehicle model information and FuelEconomy.gov for MPG estimates.
  • AAA: regional gasoline-price information used for estimates.
  • Google Fonts and Wikimedia: fonts and any place imagery requested by applicable app screens.

These providers handle information under their own terms and privacy policies. ROUTE does not need to send your complete recorded drive history to calculate statistics, but location-based lookups or map features can send relevant coordinates. Internet requests should not be understood as remaining entirely on your device.

Backups and sharing

If you export a backup, the file may contain sensitive location history, saved routes, vehicles and settings. You choose where to store or share it using iOS. A recipient or storage provider may then have access to that information. Restoring a backup imports its contents onto your device. Copies you export remain where you saved them until you delete them separately. Device backups managed by Apple or your computer may also include app data, depending on your backup settings.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains until you delete it, replace it through app controls, remove app data, or delete the app. You can delete individual recorded drives and manage saved routes and vehicles in ROUTE. Deleting the app rather than offloading it generally removes its local data; external backups, exports and provider logs are not removed by that action. ROUTE cannot erase records independently retained by third-party providers.

Advertising, sales and analytics

ROUTE does not sell your drive history, use advertising identifiers, serve paid ads, or include a developer analytics or advertising SDK. External service requests and their operational logs are separate from this statement. Apple may process App Store, diagnostic or usage information according to your device settings and Apple's policies.

Security

ROUTE relies on iOS app storage protections and uses HTTPS for external web requests. No storage or transmission method is guaranteed to be completely secure. Protect your device and exported backups, which can reveal places you have visited.

Children

ROUTE is not specifically directed at children under 13. It has no developer-managed user accounts or social features. If you believe personal information concerning a child has been sent directly to the developer, contact the address below.
