This Privacy Notice for Route Tracker (“we,” “us,” or “our”) describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share (“process”) your personal information when you use our services (“Services”), including when you:

  • Download and use our mobile application (Route Tracker), or any other application of ours that links to this Privacy Notice
  • Engage with us in other related ways, including sales, marketing, or events

Questions or concerns? Reading this Privacy Notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. We are responsible for making decisions about how your personal information is processed. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you still have any questions or concerns, please contact us at hello@route.jetzt.

Summary of key points

  • What information is covered? Account and profile information, saved trips, routes, vehicles, and app preferences. See what we collect.
  • Can you control recording? You can turn off automatic recording in the app and manage location and motion access in iPhone Settings. See your permission choices.
  • Can you delete your account? Yes. Start in Profile → Account → Delete account. See what deletion covers.

1. What information do we collect?

  • Account and profile. Your email address, account identifier, name or username, and optional profile photo. Supabase handles email and password authentication.
  • Recorded drives. Precise GPS coordinates and timestamps, start and end locations, distance, duration, and trip notes you add.
  • Routes, vehicles and settings. Saved routes, destination searches, recent places, vehicle details, fuel economy estimates, and preferences you enter or select.
  • Support and service logs. If you contact us, we receive the contact details and information you choose to send. Providers may receive connection and request details, such as your IP address, when you use online features or visit this page.

2. How do we use your information?

We use account and profile information to sign you in, verify your email, help you recover access, and associate saved information with your account. We use route and vehicle details to plan trips and estimate costs. Your recorded drives provide the trip history and driving statistics you see in the app.

When you’re signed in, saved trips, including their precise GPS paths, sync to your Route Tracker account through Supabase. Your routes, vehicles, profile information, and preferences can also sync so the app can restore them. Offline changes may remain on your iPhone until they sync.

3. When do we share your information?

  • Supabase provides authentication and stores the synced account data described above.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and Apple Maps services. Maps, routing, place lookup, and fuel-station searches may use search terms and coordinates. Opening directions in Apple Maps passes route details to that app.
  • Other providers may process searches, coordinates, routing preferences, or planned departure times. These include OpenStreetMap-based services such as Photon and Nominatim, and Open-Meteo for elevation. NHTSA, FuelEconomy.gov, and AAA provide vehicle or regional data for estimates. Some screens may request fonts or imagery from Google Fonts or Wikimedia. Providers may receive connection details and retain logs under their own policies.

We require service providers processing personal data on our behalf to protect it consistently with this policy and applicable requirements. Route Tracker does not sell your drive history or use it for targeted advertising. If you share an exported backup, the recipient or storage service you choose can access the information it contains.

4. How do location and motion permissions work?

With your permission, Route Tracker uses location for the live map, route planning, distance measurements, and recorded drive paths. When you enable drive recording and allow background location, the app can use location while the screen is locked or another app is open. With Motion & Fitness permission, motion signals help the app recognize vehicle travel for automatic recording.

You can turn off automatic recording in the app or change Location, Precise Location, and Motion & Fitness permissions in iPhone Settings. Route planning supports a manually entered address. Changing permissions limits future use. Saved trips remain until you delete them.

5. What happens when you sign in with Apple?

If you sign in with Apple, Apple provides an account identifier and may share your name and email address or a private relay address. We use this information to create or sign you in to your Route Tracker account. You may be asked to confirm with Apple when you request deletion.

6. How long do we keep your information?

We keep active account information and synced records while your account is open unless you delete them sooner. Service backups and operational logs may retain data until their applicable retention periods expire. We may also retain information when required by law. You manage any copies in your own exports or device backups separately.

7. How do we protect your information?

Route Tracker uses iOS storage protections, keeps sign-in sessions in the iOS Keychain, and uses encrypted connections for online services. Cloud access controls associate synced records with the signed-in account. Exports can contain precise location history, so choose carefully where you store or share them.

8. Do we collect information from children?

Route Tracker is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information to us.

9. What privacy choices do you have?

Where applicable, you can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, or withdraw consent. Use iPhone Settings to change location and motion access, or contact us to make a privacy request. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

10. How do we update this policy?

We’ll publish revised policy text and the effective date on this page.

11. How can you contact us?

Route Tracker is developed by Heather Davies. For privacy questions or requests, email violet6889@gmail.com.

12. How do you review, update, or delete your data?

You can update your profile in the app, delete individual drives, and manage saved routes and vehicles. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data, subject to device backups. Your account and cloud copy remain until you delete them.

To request account deletion, open Profile → Account → Delete account and confirm. When deletion completes, Route Tracker removes your account and its saved app data from active cloud storage, clears that account’s app data from the iPhone used to request deletion, and signs you out. Exported files and device backups are managed separately. If you can’t complete your request in the app, contact us for help.
