Cover Compass

Making auto insurance quotes easier to compare.

Making sense of the details

Cover Compass is an early web app that helps people organize auto insurance quotes they have received and compare the details side by side. It highlights missing or different coverage information so users can identify questions to ask an insurer.

Users can enter quotes, choose whether price or deductible matters most, review coverage differences, and explore the trade-offs. Sample quotes are clearly labeled; the app does not retrieve live prices or determine eligibility.

What comes next

The next step is to test whether people can enter their quotes, notice coverage differences, and identify missing information. The app has not yet collected usability results or usage analytics.
